# Introduction to the Physical AI Book

Welcome to the fascinating world of Physical AI. This book is a journey into the heart of embodied intelligence, exploring how we can build machines that don't just think, but also act, perceive, and learn in the physical world.

## Who is this book for?

This book is for aspiring roboticists, AI practitioners curious about embodiment, and anyone interested in the future of intelligent systems. Whether you are a student, a hobbyist, or a professional developer, you will find practical knowledge and foundational concepts to help you get started with Physical AI.

## What you will learn

We will start from the ground up, covering the core concepts and tools that power modern robotics and Physical AI.

*   **Chapter 1: Introduction to Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence:** We'll define what Physical AI is and why having a body is crucial for developing true intelligence.
*   **Chapter 2: The Robotics Nervous System (ROS 2 Basics):** You'll be introduced to the Robot Operating System (ROS 2), the essential software framework for most modern robots.
*   **Chapter 3: ROS 2 In-Depth:** We'll take a deeper, code-focused dive into the main communication patterns in ROS 2.
*   **Chapter 4: Building ROS 2 Packages:** Learn how to structure, build, and run your robotics software in a professional and scalable way.

## Let's Begin

The journey to creating intelligent physical agents is challenging, but immensely rewarding. Let's take the first step together.
